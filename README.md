## `research`

### Anti-Cheat Compatibility on Apple Silicon
**Toward Closing the Anti-Cheat Gap on macOS**

Co-authored with **NZ-Linix**. I contributed to the research and a small part of the implementation.

The paper explores the compatibility problem posed by Easy Anti-Cheat on Apple Silicon, including virtualization, Linux/Proton compatibility, x86-64 translation, and the boundaries between compatibility and anti-cheat behavior.

**[Read the paper →](https://cdn.nzlinix.com/share/papers/eac-apple-silicon.pdf)**
